# TELTONIKA RELEVANCE
 
## Purpose
 
Help determine whether company news, projects or activities discovered during research have a meaningful connection to Teltonika Networks.
 
The objective is NOT to recommend a specific Teltonika product.
 
The objective is to identify:
**"Could this development create a credible reason for Irmantas to start a conversation with this company?"**
 
---
 
## 1. Core Teltonika Relevance
 
A development is potentially relevant when it involves physical equipment or infrastructure that may require:
 
- Cellular connectivity (4G/LTE/5G)
- Internet/WAN connectivity
- Remote connectivity
- Networking between devices
- Remote monitoring or management
- Connectivity redundancy/failover
- Wi-Fi
- Ethernet networking
- IoT/M2M communication
- Connecting legacy/industrial equipment
- Vehicle/mobile connectivity
- Starlink + cellular connectivity
- Secure remote access
- Edge connectivity
 
The presence of physical/distributed equipment is particularly important.

## 3. Strong Relevance Signals
 
Give higher importance when research shows:
 
- Large number of distributed assets/sites
- Equipment operating remotely
- New infrastructure rollout
- New fleet/equipment deployment
- Existing use of cellular networking
- Connectivity is operationally important
- Remote monitoring/control is required
- Starlink deployment
- Connectivity redundancy requirements
- Existing networking/IoT project
- Replacement or upgrade of existing communications infrastructure
 
---
 
## 4. Relevance ≠ Confirmed Opportunity
 
Never assume:
 
**"They have remote sites → they need Teltonika."**
 
Instead:
 
**FACT:** Company is deploying 150 remote solar installations.
 
**INFERENCE:** Distributed sites may require remote communications/monitoring.
 
**OUTREACH RELEVANCE:** Worth exploring how connectivity is being handled across those sites.
 
This distinction is critical.
 
---
 
## 5. Conversation-First Approach
 
Teltonika relevance should normally lead to a QUESTION, not a product pitch.
 
Prefer:
 
**"It could be worth understanding how they are handling connectivity across these new sites."**
 
Not:
 
**"They should use RUTC50 routers."**
 
Initial outreach should aim to:
1. Demonstrate genuine knowledge of what the company is doing.
2. Establish potential relevance.
3. Start a conversation.
4. Learn their actual requirements.
 
Product selection comes later.
 
---
 
## 6. Weak Relevance
 
Do not force relevance from:
 
- Industry alone
- Generic company growth
- Hiring
- Revenue growth
- Generic digital transformation
- Cloud/software projects with no physical connectivity angle
- Projects where networking relevance is purely speculative
 
It is acceptable to conclude:
 
**"No meaningful Teltonika angle found."**
