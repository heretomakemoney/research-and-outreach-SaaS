SALES TRIGGERS
 
## Purpose
 
Identify which researched developments create the best reason for Irmantas to contact a company.
 
Companies provided by Irmantas are already considered potential Teltonika targets.
 
The goal is:
 
**"What is the best available reason or hook to contact this company now?"**
 
A trigger does NOT need to prove that the company needs Teltonika.
 
---
 
## 1. Trigger Types
 
Distinguish between:
 
### SALES TRIGGER
Evidence suggesting a potential Teltonika-related opportunity.
 
### CONVERSATION HOOK
Recent, genuine information that helps personalize outreach but does not itself indicate a Teltonika opportunity.
 
Both are useful.
 
Always prefer a strong Sales Trigger. If none exists, find the best credible Conversation Hook.
 
---
 
## 2. HIGH PRIORITY Triggers
 
Strong reasons to contact now:
 
- New relevant project or contract
- Infrastructure/equipment rollout
- Connectivity/networking/IoT deployment
- 4G/5G deployment
- Starlink deployment
- New sites, fleet or distributed assets
- Remote monitoring/automation project
- CCTV/security rollout
- EV charging rollout
- Digital signage/kiosk/ticketing rollout
- Relevant tender or upcoming project
- Technology modernization
- Connectivity/reliability problem
- Relevant outage
- Connectivity/networking upgrade
- Competitor technology being reviewed, deployed or replaced
- Expansion of remote/distributed infrastructure
 
---
 
## 3. MEDIUM PRIORITY Triggers
 
Useful when direct Teltonika relevance is less clear:
 
- Company expansion
- New facility
- Expansion into new region
- Major new customer
- Acquisition
- Relevant partnership
- Hiring in networking/IoT/OT
- Digital transformation
- Investment in operational technology
- New technology strategy
- Major funding/investment linked to expansion
 
Investigate whether these developments involve physical infrastructure, networking, connectivity or connected equipment before assuming Teltonika relevance.
 
---
 
## 4. LOW PRIORITY / Conversation Hooks
 
These may still be useful for personalizing outreach:
 
- Executive appointment
- Company milestone
- Award/certification
- General company growth
- New office/location
- Sustainability initiative
- Broader company announcement
- Older but still relevant project
- Other meaningful recent company news
 
These do NOT automatically indicate a Teltonika opportunity.
 
Use them as natural conversation openers when no stronger trigger exists.
 
Never pretend they have Teltonika relevance when none is supported.
 
---
 
## 5. Trigger Evaluation
 
Evaluate potential triggers based on:
 
### Recency
Prefer:
- <3 months = strongest
- 3–6 months = strong
- 6–12 months = usable
- >12 months = usually background unless still active
 
### Specificity
Concrete projects/events are more valuable than broad corporate announcements.
 
### Teltonika Relevance
Is there credible connectivity, networking, IoT or connected-infrastructure relevance?
 
### Scale
Look for:
- Number of sites
- Vehicles
- Devices/assets
- Locations
- Project value
- Geographic rollout
- Deployment timeframe
 
Larger or repeatable deployments deserve more attention.
 
### Actionability
Can Irmantas naturally reference this information when contacting someone?
 
---
 
## 6. Trigger Ranking
 
When multiple findings exist, rank them:
 
**PRIMARY TRIGGER**
Best reason to contact the company now.
 
**SECONDARY TRIGGERS**
Other useful developments.
 
**CONVERSATION HOOKS**
Useful personalization when stronger triggers are unavailable.
 
Do not combine unrelated weak findings to manufacture a strong opportunity.
 
---
 
## 7. Convert Trigger Into Conversation
 
Triggers should create a reason to talk, not force a product pitch.
 
Example:
 
**FACT**
Company announced deployment of 300 new EV charging locations.
 
**TELTONIKA RELEVANCE**
Distributed chargers may require connectivity and remote management.
 
**CONVERSATION ANGLE**
Understand how connectivity is being handled across the rollout.
 
**AVOID**
"Teltonika has the perfect router for your chargers."
 
The objective of initial outreach is to start a conversation and learn more.
 
---
 
## 8. Existing / Ongoing Projects
 
Do not automatically reject older news.
 
An older project may still be valuable if it is:
 
- Still being deployed
- Expanding
- Multi-year
- Entering another phase
- Likely to require ongoing procurement
 
Research its current status before using it.
 
---
 
## 9. When No Strong Trigger Exists
 
Do NOT abandon an account because no strong Teltonika-related trigger was found.
 
Use this hierarchy:
 
1. Strong Teltonika-related Sales Trigger
2. Medium relevance Sales Trigger
3. Genuine recent Conversation Hook
4. Relevant older company development
5. Generic outreach only if nothing useful exists
 
Clearly identify the strength/type of the finding.
 
Never manufacture Teltonika relevance simply to justify outreach.
 
---
 
## 10. Continuous Improvement
 
When Irmantas identifies that a particular trigger consistently produces good or bad outreach opportunities, ask:
 
**"This looks like a reusable sales-trigger rule. Should I update `03_SALES_TRIGGERS.md`?"**
 
Also suggest updates when repeated research reveals a new trigger category worth monitoring.
 
Only update after Irmantas approves.
