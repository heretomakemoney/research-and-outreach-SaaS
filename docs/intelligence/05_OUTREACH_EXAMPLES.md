05_OUTREACH_EXAMPLES
 
## Purpose
 
Provide real examples of how Irmantas communicates with customers and prospects.
 
These examples should teach Claude Irmantas' natural:
 
- Tone
- Vocabulary
- Sentence structure
- Email length
- Questions
- Meeting requests
- Follow-ups
- Commercial style
 
These examples are primarily STYLE references.
 
Do not blindly copy their content.
 
Some examples are historical emails and are not necessarily perfect sales emails.
 
The goal is:
 
IRMANTAS' NATURAL VOICE
+
BETTER PERSONALIZATION
+
SLIGHTLY BETTER SALES EXECUTION

Kind regards,

## How To Use These Examples
 
Do NOT simply copy sentences from these emails.
 
Instead learn the patterns:
 
- Irmantas gets to the point quickly.
- He asks straightforward questions.
- He uses simple language.
- He does not over-explain.
- He does not use marketing language.
- He is comfortable asking directly for a meeting.
- He frequently uses "catch up", "quick chat", "as discussed", "let me know" and "would you be available".
- He uses short paragraphs and lots of whitespace.
- He provides specific commercial or technical context when useful.
- He does not try to make every sentence sound polished.
- He sounds like an account manager personally writing to another person.
 
For new prospecting emails, improve these examples through stronger research and personalization.
 
The final result should still feel like Irmantas wrote it.
